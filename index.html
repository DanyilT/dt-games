Bounce

How to play

Roll the red ball through every hoop in the level: then the door (the exit, striped red) opens, and the ball goes through it to the next level. Spikes pop the ball, and so do the moving spikes.

A crystal is where the ball comes back after a pop, and a crystal ball is a spare ball. Rubber floors bounce the ball higher. A pump makes the ball big (big balls float in water, small ones sink, and some hoops are too small for them), and a deflater makes it small again. Power-ups last 12 seconds: speed, a higher jump, and gravity upside down.

On a touch screen, the keypad takes several fingers at once: hold 6 and 2 together to jump to the right. On the menus, tap what you want.

The game is saved when the menu opens and at the start of each level, so it carries on next time.
