How to Play:

Get three of your marks in a row (across, down or corner to corner) before the bot does.

A match is 5 rounds. X always goes first: you're X in rounds 1, 3 and 5, and O in rounds 2 and 4.

A round won is 2 points, a draw 1. Most points after 5 rounds wins the match.

Easy and Medium slip up now and then. Hard never does: a draw is the best you can get there.

Click or tap a square, or press 1 to 9 (1 is the top left, 9 the bottom right). The arrow keys move between the squares, and Enter plays one.

Press "N" for a new match, and "E", "M" or "H" for the level.
