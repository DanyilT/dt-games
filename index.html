How to Play:

Press Space (or ↑, or W), click or tap to flap: each flap lifts the bird, and it falls in between.

Fly through the gaps between the pipes. Each pipe you pass is a point.

Don't touch a pipe or the ground!

Medals: bronze from 10 points, silver from 20, gold from 30, platinum from 40.

Press "P" or "Esc" (or the button at the top left of the game) to pause. A paused game is saved: close the page, and it carries on next time.

Press "R" to go back to Get Ready, and "M" to turn the sound on or off.
