About Solitaire

How to play Solitaire

Move all 52 cards onto the four foundations at the top right, each suit from the ace up to the king.

The table

  • On the seven columns, build down in alternating colours: a red 6 on a black 7. You can move a whole run of face-up cards at once, and a face-down card left on top turns over by itself.
  • Only a king, or a run starting with one, goes into an empty column.
  • Click the stock (top left) to deal 1 card, or 3, onto the waste. When it's empty, click it to turn the waste over and go through again.
  • When there are no more useful moves, the game is lost.

Scoring (Game › Options)

  • Standard: 10 points for each card onto a foundation, 5 for a card from the waste onto a column or a card turned over, −15 for a card taken back off a foundation. Each new pass through the deck costs 100 with Draw one, and 20 after the third with Draw three. In a timed game you lose 2 points every 10 seconds, and a win that takes more than 30 seconds earns a bonus of 700,000 ÷ its seconds.
  • Vegas: each game costs $52 and every card onto a foundation wins $5. One pass through the deck with Draw one, three with Draw three. With Cumulative, your dollars carry on from game to game.
  • No scoring: just the time.
