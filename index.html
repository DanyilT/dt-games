About Minesweeper

How to Play Minesweeper

Open every cell that doesn't hide a mine.

Mouse
Left-clickOpen a cell
Right-clickPlace or take off a flag
Click a numberOpen the cells round it, once its mines are flagged
Touch
TapOpen a cell (or flag it, in flag mode)
Long-tapFlag a cell (or open it, in flag mode)
The flag buttonFlag mode on or off (it's next to Help)
Keyboard
↑ ← ↓ →Move the selection
SpaceOpen the selected cell
Shift + SpaceFlag the selected cell
NNew game
MFlag mode on or off
1 2 3Beginner, Intermediate, Expert
IShow or hide these instructions
EscThe Game menu (or close a dialog, or a menu)
Tab EnterThe Game and Help menus: Tab to one, Enter to open it, the arrows to choose, Enter to pick

Tips

  • A number tells you how many mines are in the cells round it.
  • Flag the cells you're sure hide a mine.
  • The counter at the top left shows the mines, less your flags.
